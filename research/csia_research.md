## 1. Executive Summary & Identity

- **Official Name:** **Cybersecurity Intelligence Alliance** (abbreviated as **CSIA**)
- **Mascot / Member Moniker:** **"Cyberfoxes"**
- **Academic Programs Represented:**
  - Bachelor of Science (BS) in Cybersecurity
  - BS in Cybersecurity + Professional Science's Master's (PSM) in Cybersecurity
  - Cross-departmental coordination with Computer Science (CS), Network Administration (NetAd), Web Development (WD), and Entertainment & Multimedia Computing (EMC).
- **Core Mission:** To embody the ideals and aspirations of cybersecurity students, uphold university integrity, foster ethical white-hat hacking principles, and develop the next generation of cybersecurity defenders.

## 3. Organizational Objectives & Purpose (Article II)

1. **Student Representation:** Serve as the democratic representative body for BS and BS+PSM Cybersecurity students at Holy Angel University.
2. **HAU Core Values:** Embody Christ-centeredness, integrity, excellence, community, and societal responsibility.
3. **Skill Empowerment:** Organize technical seminars, workshops, CTFs, and wargames to equip students with real-world industry tools.
4. **Collaborative Ecosystem:** Foster cross-discipline partnerships with internal university programs and external industry organizations (such as **UnitDEVCON**).
5. **Industry Pipeline:** Produce job-ready ethical cybersecurity professionals, penetration testers, SOC analysts, and security engineers.

## 4. Distinctive Traditions & Member Protocols

### The "Cyberfox Codename" Protocol (Article III, Section 5)
A standout professional and subcultural element of CSIA:
- Every Cyberfox is assigned/chooses a **Codename** to simulate operational anonymity, build an ethical hacker persona, and foster privacy.
- **Specifications:**
  - Length: 3 to 10 characters.
  - Formatting: All lowercase (`small caps`), single word, no special characters, not all digits.
  - Used in: Internal events, CTF leaderboards, external hackathons, and wargame competitions.

### Membership & Eligibility
- Open to all bona fide students under Holy Angel University's School of Computing (SoC), specifically those in the Cybersecurity programs.
- Term of membership: Valid per semester with active renewal.

## 5. Organizational Structure & Leadership Hierarchy

### A. Executive Directorate
- **President:** Chief executive, official representative to external stakeholders, supervises meetings, event planning, and policy enforcement.
- **Vice President for Internal:** Oversees internal operational excellence, interdepartmental synergy, and disciplinary hearings.
- **Vice President for External:** Spearheads foreign relations, strategic partnerships, sponsor liaisons, and public events.
- **Secretary:** Oversees secretariat, official documentation, scheduling, and archival systems.
- **Adviser:** Faculty/industry professional providing strategic counsel, intelligence oversight, and organizational guidance.

### B. Technical, Operational & Event Branches
- **Technical Director & Staff:** Manages lab hardware, network infrastructure, cybersecurity testing devices, troubleshooting, and lab tooling.
- **Events Director & Staff:** Plans, designs, and executes hackathons, conferences, technical seminars, and workshops.
- **Logistics Director & Staff:** Manages asset inventory, vendor procurement, equipment custodial duties, and venue coordination.

### C. Financial & Audit Branch
- **Finance Director & Staff:** Long-term budget strategy, risk mitigation, and financial controls.
- **Accounting Director & Staff:** Day-to-day bookkeeping, accounts reconciliations, ledger maintenance, and audits.

### D. Public Relations, Creatives & Program Coordinators
- **Public Relations Director:** Institutional communications, social media channels, and media liaisons.
- **Multimedia and Creatives Director & Staff:** Visual branding, publication materials, video production, and event assets.
- **Program Relations Coordinators:** Dedicated liaisons representing other SoC student tracks:
  - *NetAd Relations Coordinator* (Network Administration)
  - *CS Relations Coordinator* (Computer Science)
  - *WebDev Relations Coordinator* (Web Development)
  - *EMC Relations Coordinator* (Entertainment & Multimedia Computing)

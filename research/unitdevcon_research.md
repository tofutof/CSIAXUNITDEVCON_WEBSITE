# UnitDEVCON // Community & Ecosystem Research Dossier

> **Founding Synergy:** A grassroots collaboration between **DEVCON** (Developers Connect Philippines / DEVCON Pampanga) and **UNIT2000**.  
> **Headquarters / Base:** **Angeles City, Pampanga, Philippines**  
> **Core Ethos:** **"Cybersecurity Not Gatekept"** — Free, open-access, grassroots cybersecurity education.  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Genesis

**UnitDEVCON** is a collaborative technology and cybersecurity movement born in Angeles City, Pampanga, merging the developer reach of **DEVCON Philippines** (the largest community of developers and tech practitioners in the Philippines) with **UNIT2000** (a specialized community of cybersecurity researchers, offensive operators, and defense practitioners).

While traditional cybersecurity education is notoriously expensive, certificate-heavy, and heavily gatekept behind paywalls and corporate NDAs, **UnitDEVCON** was founded on the fundamental principle that:
> *"Cybersecurity skills, defense blueprints, and offensive tradecraft must be accessible to everyone for free — zero gatekeeping, 100% community empowerment."*

---

## 2. The Collaborative Pillars

### A. DEVCON (Developers Connect Philippines / DEVCON Pampanga)
- **Background:** The premier non-profit tech organization in the Philippines, uniting software engineers, devops practitioners, students, and tech enthusiasts through regional chapters.
- **Contribution:** Developer culture, extensive community organizing muscle, nationwide network, mentorship culture, and hackathon infrastructure.

### B. UNIT2000
- **Background:** A grassroots cybersecurity and hacker collective rooted in Angeles City/Central Luzon.
- **Contribution:** Deep technical infosec expertise, hands-on red team/blue team/purple team labs, CTF challenges, threat hunting, and security governance (GRC).

### C. The Joint Mission: UnitDEVCON
- **Free Hands-on Workshops:** Live interactive sessions covering network exploitation, web security, reverse engineering, digital forensics, and secure coding.
- **Convergence Series:** Multi-track gatherings (e.g., *"Convergence: From Policy to People"*, security governance, purple teaming).
- **Zero Gatekeeping:** Removing financial, institutional, and elitist barriers to entry for aspiring ethical hackers and software builders.
